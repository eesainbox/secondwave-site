Anchor: id="faqs"
Headline: Good questions deserve straight answers.
Accordion. Full-width tap targets. Smooth expand and collapse.

Q1: Why is SecondWave Inbox different?
A: Most agencies sell you on their process. We remove the risk of it entirely. You pay upfront for two weeks of real work. On day 14 you have assets running in your account and data to judge us by. Not a deck. Not a PDF. Actual work, first.

Q2: How does payment work?
A: The two-week pilot is paid in full before we start. That payment initiates the build. After 14 days you have the assets, the data, and a clear picture of what moved. From there you decide what comes next. No pressure. No auto-renewal.

Q3: Do I keep the work if I don't continue?
A: Yes. Everything we build lives in your Klaviyo account. You own it from day one.

Q4: What do you need from me to get started?
A: Klaviyo collaborator or admin access, your existing brand guidelines or templates, and 30 minutes for a kickoff call. We handle the rest.

Q5: Do you work with brands outside Canada?
A: Yes. Canada, US, UK, Australia. Klaviyo works everywhere we do.
